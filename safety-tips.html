Disaster Safety Guidelines

Remember these important precautions.

🌊

Flood Safety

  • Move to higher ground when advised.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from drains and fast-flowing water.
  • Keep drinking water and essential medicines ready.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions.
🌎

Earthquake Safety

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On during shaking.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • Do not use elevators during an earthquake.
  • After shaking stops, check for hazards and leave damaged buildings carefully.
🔥

Fire Safety

  • Raise the alarm and leave the building quickly.
  • Use stairs, not elevators.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Never re-enter a burning building.
  • Call emergency services from a safe place.
🌀

Cyclone Safety

  • Monitor official weather warnings.
  • Secure loose outdoor objects if it is safe.
  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Evacuate when instructed by authorities.
  • Avoid fallen power lines and flooded roads.

BE READY

Emergency Kit Checklist

Keep essential supplies in an easy-to-carry bag.

💧 Drinking water
🥫 Non-perishable food
🔦 Torch and spare batteries
🩹 First-aid kit
💊 Essential medicines
📱 Charged phone and power bank
📄 Copies of important documents
📻 Battery-powered radio, if available

REMEMBER

Your Safety Comes First.

Stay calm, avoid unnecessary risks and follow instructions from emergency responders and local authorities.
